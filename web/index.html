Team Allocation Simulator

Upload a cohort of students and this runs the allocator in your browser: it sorts each tutorial group by CGPA, interleaves the gender split into teams of five, then swaps students until team averages sit inside the group’s CGPA band and no team carries three from the same school.

Port of src/allocator.py · runs entirely client-side · nothing is uploaded anywhere

Constraint 1 · Gender 3M2F or 2M3F Both genders present in every team; skewed splits only when the group forces them.
Constraint 2 · CGPA ±0.20 of group mean Measured against each tutorial group’s own mean, not the cohort mean.
Constraint 3 · School Max 2 per school Swap candidates must match gender and sit within ±0.50 CGPA.

Thresholds

STEP 2
±0.20
How far a team average may sit from its tutorial group mean before the allocator tries to swap it back.
±0.50
A student can only be moved out of an over-represented school if a same-gender peer sits within this CGPA distance.

Expected format

Tutorial Group,Student ID,School,Name,Gender,CGPA
G-1,S001,EEE,Alex Tan,Male,4.20
G-1,S002,CoB (NBS),Ravi Kumar,Female,3.85
G-1,S003,SCSE,Mei Lin,Female,4.51
…
  • Header row required; six columns in that order.
  • Gender is Male or Female. CGPA is a number.
  • Groups are read from column 1 — any number of groups, any size from 5 students up.
  • Quoted fields with commas are handled.
